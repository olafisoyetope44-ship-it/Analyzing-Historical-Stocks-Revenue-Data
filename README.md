# Historical Stock and Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores historical stock prices and company revenue using Python, data extraction, web scraping and data visualisation.

The objective is to investigate how the historical share prices and reported revenue of Tesla and GameStop change over time, and to communicate the findings through visual analysis.

## Business Questions

- How have Tesla and GameStop share prices changed over time?
- How has reported quarterly revenue changed?
- What patterns can be observed when comparing share prices and revenue?
- How can financial data be collected, cleaned and presented to support further analysis?

## Analysis Workflow

1. **Data collection:** Retrieve historical stock price information and collect company revenue data.
2. **Data preparation:** Clean, transform and organise the datasets for analysis.
3. **Exploratory analysis:** Examine historical trends and changes in stock prices and revenue.
4. **Visualisation:** Present the data through charts that make trends easier to interpret.
5. **Interpretation:** Summarise observations, limitations and possible business implications.

## Limitations

Historical share prices and reported revenue measure different aspects of company performance. A visual relationship between them does not establish causation or demonstrate that a stock is a good investment.

Data availability, reporting periods and changes to external data sources may affect reproducibility.
